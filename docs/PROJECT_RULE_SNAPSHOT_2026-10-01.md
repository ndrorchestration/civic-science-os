## External generative-AI boundary

Source: https://www.zooniverse.org/about/ai-ethics

Zooniverse's current FAQ states that volunteers should not use external generative-AI tools such as ChatGPT or Gemini to aid classifications unless explicit permission has been granted.

For this repository's first pilot, no such project-specific permission has been recorded. Therefore the controlling rule remains:

`classification_mode=HUMAN_ONLY`
`ai_assistance_during_classification=false`

## Allowed AI assistance in this OS

AI may assist before or after the classification session with:
- timestamps and session metadata;
- provenance/source capture;
- schema validation;
- ambiguity-code organization;
- process-review generation;
- software testing and documentation.

AI may not:
- inspect or interpret a subject/light curve for the contributor;
- recommend a classification;
- automate classification clicks or submissions;
- relabel an AI-produced judgment as a human observation.

## Claim ceiling

This snapshot verifies a participation-rule boundary and current project availability only. It does not establish scientific contribution, classification skill, project endorsement, or authorization to automate classifications.
